Delta Green Enhanced Combat HUD

A combat surface for Delta Green that puts everything an Agent needs during a
firefight one click away, and applies the results -- without changing how Delta
Green's dice work.

Requires Argon - Combat HUD (CORE). This module contributes Delta Green
components to Argon and does not render a HUD of its own.

- Portrait and vitals: HP, WP, Sanity, Breaking Point and armour. Sanity honours
  the system's Keep Sanity Private setting.
- Attacks: every equipped weapon, with damage, Lethality, armour piercing, range
  and ammunition. Right-click asks whether to roll damage or Lethality, using the
  system's own dialog.
- Skills: every skill the system defines, including typed skills and Special
  Training, grouped by trained and untrained.
- Reactions: Dodge and Fight Back, shown only when the Agent has the skill.
- Sanity tests, and an optional Willpower Boost house rule (off by default).
- Agent Record drawer: statistics, Bonds and Motivations.
- Damage automation: after a damage or Lethality roll against a target, the
  result is resolved through armour and offered in chat with an Apply button.
  Choose Propose or Auto-apply in the module settings.

Every roll goes through the Delta Green system unchanged, so modifier dialogs,
chat cards and Dice So Nice behave exactly as they do on the character sheet.
